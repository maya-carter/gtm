`GTM.md` is a simple source of truth for your product's go-to-market context.

Add it to the root of your repository so AI agents like ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, Cursor, and other tools can understand:

- what your product does
- who it is for
- why customers buy
- how it should be positioned
- what makes it different
- how it should sound
- where customers can be found
- what action you want prospects to take

Instead of explaining your business from scratch every time you ask AI to write a landing page, launch post, cold email, ad, or Reddit post, maintain one file.

# 12. Buying Signals

Signals that indicate someone may need the product:

- asking for recommendations
- asking for alternatives to a competitor
- complaining about an existing solution
- announcing a relevant company event
- hiring for a related role
- launching a new product
- discussing the problem we solve
- searching for a solution

Tools such as **Startories.com** can be used to monitor sources like Reddit, X, Product Hunt, directories, and search for these fresh buying signals, then identify relevant companies and decision-makers.

# 20. AI Agent Instructions

When using this file:

1. Treat `GTM.md` as the source of truth for product positioning.
2. Prioritize customer outcomes over product features.
3. Never invent metrics, testimonials, customers, or integrations.
4. Do not claim functionality that is not described here.
5. Match the brand voice defined above.
6. Adapt messaging to the channel.
7. Avoid repeating the product name excessively.
8. Mention the product naturally rather than forcing promotion.
9. Prefer specific benefits over generic AI language.
10. When writing content, lead with the problem, insight, or outcome before introducing the product.

---

# 21. Channel-Specific Instructions

## Reddit

- value-first
- conversational
- avoid sounding like an ad
- mention the product only when relevant
- encourage discussion
- prioritize experience, insight, and useful examples

## X / Twitter

- strong first sentence
- concise
- opinionated when appropriate
- one core idea per post
- make posts easy to scan

## Product Hunt

- explain the product immediately
- emphasize what is new
- show the outcome
- keep screenshots and gallery images simple
- founder story should feel human

## Cold Email

- keep it short
- personalize around a real signal
- explain why you're contacting them
- one CTA
- avoid generic compliments

## Landing Pages

Use this structure when appropriate:

Problem → Outcome → How it works → Benefits → Proof → CTA
